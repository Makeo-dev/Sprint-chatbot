HOTEL FAQ 

Check-in time: Standard check-in is at 6:00 AM. Early check-in from 11:00 AM depends on room availability and may attract a fee of ₦15,000.
Check-out time: Standard check-out is at 12:00 PM. Late check-out until 4:00 PM may be requested at reception (subject to availability and a ₦20,000 fee).
Breakfast: Served daily from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM at The Palm Terrace Restaurant; complimentary for Deluxe, Executive, and Suite rates (₦8,500 per person for Standard room guests).
Wi-Fi: Complimentary high-speed fiber Wi-Fi (AuraCrest_Guest) is available in all rooms and public areas. Network name and password are on the welcome card in your room.
Payment: Bookings are paid online through Paystack by debit/credit card or bank transfer; walk-in incidentals can be settled by card, transfer, or cash at the front desk.
Cancellation: Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before 2:00 PM on your check-in date; cancellations within 48 hours incur a 50% first-night charge.
Airport pickup: Available on request via our hotel shuttle from Murtala Muhammed International/Domestic Airport (₦25,000 sedan / ₦40,000 SUV); please book at least 6 hours ahead through reception or the Requests page.
Parking: Complimentary 24-hour secured on-site parking is available for registered guests (1 vehicle per room; extra vehicles subject to space).
Room service: Available daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM through the Requests page (a 10% service charge applies to food and beverage orders).
Housekeeping or maintenance problem: Guests can submit a request anytime on the Requests page and track its live status there; urgent plumbing, AC, or power issues are prioritized within 30 minutes.
Reception: Dial 0 from your room phone, message us on the platform, or call +234-809-555-0194 (24/7 front desk).
